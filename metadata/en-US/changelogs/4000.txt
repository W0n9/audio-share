Server 0.4.0
- The Server is rewritten in Rust and ships a tray-first GUI for Windows and Linux.
- Windows: portable AudioShareServer.exe; requires the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime.
- Linux: AudioShareServer deb and AppImage packages (Ubuntu 24.04+), plus the headless as-cmd archive.
- as-cmd keeps the v0.3.4 flags, defaults, and Wire Protocol; it now also writes server.log.
- Existing v0.3.4 config.ini files are migrated without losing settings.
- The Android Client is unchanged at v0.3.4; no new APK is included in this release.
